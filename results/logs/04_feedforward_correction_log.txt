Purpose:
Validate non-Abelian feed-forward correction for both left-acting and right-acting geometric distortions.

Synthetic holonomy:
Generated from the non-Abelian connection A(t) = i [0.7 cos(t) sigma_x + 0.4 sin(2t) sigma_y + 0.2 sigma_z].
The ODE reference is projected to the nearest unitary by polar decomposition before constructing the effective gates.

Left-acting convention:
V_eff = U_gamma V, V_corr = Uhat^dagger V_eff.

Right-acting convention:
V_eff = V U_gamma, V_corr = V_eff Uhat^dagger.

Expected behavior:
Holonomy-estimation error should decrease under mesh refinement.
Left and right correction errors should decrease under mesh refinement.
Correction fidelities should approach 1.
Unitarity residuals should remain near machine precision.
